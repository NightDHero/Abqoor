# AI RULES — Abqoor Engineering Constitution

## Purpose
This file defines the mandatory behavior rules for all AI agents (Codex or future tools) working on the Abqoor project.

---

## Codex Instructions

You MUST follow these rules at all times:

### 1. Source of Truth
- The /docs directory is the ONLY source of truth.
- Never invent product requirements.
- Never assume missing business logic.

---

### 2. No Hallucination Rule
- If a requirement is missing, STOP and report it.
- Do NOT guess architecture, UX, or business rules.

---

### 3. Product Integrity Rule
- Do NOT change adaptive learning logic unless explicitly instructed.
- Do NOT modify question behavior without specification.
- Do NOT alter user experience flow without documentation.

---

### 4. Minimal Change Principle
- Only modify what the task explicitly requires.
- Avoid refactoring unrelated systems.

---

### 5. Architecture Stability
- Maintain modular structure.
- Do not introduce unnecessary frameworks or complexity.
